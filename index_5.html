    We use AI the way a restaurant uses salt: in everything, slightly too much, and you will be charged for it.

    Can I match with someone in Oakland?

    Long-distance relationships are not supported at this time. The East Bay is on our roadmap for H2, pending a bridge.

    Are you profitable?

    We are focused on growth. Profit is a lagging indicator of a lack of ambition.

    How many successful couples have you produced?
